I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Oregon, advised by [Prof. Thien Huu Nguyen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Da2FhegAAAAJ&hl=vi).

My research focuses on **efficient and scalable generative modeling**, particularly for Large Language Models (LLMs). I am interested in developing **novel model architectures, training paradigms, and inference algorithms** that improve computational efficiency, long-context modeling, and reasoning capabilities. More broadly, my goal is to advance foundation models that scale effectively with compute and data across both training and deployment.

During my PhD, I have worked as a Student Researcher at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/) and a Research Scientist Intern at [Adobe Research](https://research.adobe.com/). Previously, I spent two years as an AI Resident at VinAI Research (now [Qualcomm AI Research](https://www.qualcomm.com/research/artificial-intelligence/ai-residency-program)).
